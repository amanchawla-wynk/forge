# Forge Rubric Audit Against the Recommendations PRD

## 1. Which proposed requirements are already represented, and which belong to deep review rather than scoring?

### Takeaway

Most ordinary PRD-readiness requirements are already represented in `prd.v0.yaml`. The material gap is not another broad checklist: it is the difference between evidence that a topic exists and evidence that multiple rules are mutually implementable. Forge intentionally assigns the first question to the rubric and the second to the advisory D-045/D-046 review.

### Inferences

- Do not add a scored `contradiction count`, `ranking consistency`, or `deep-review severity` criterion. That would collapse the intentional boundary between readiness evidence and advisory findings before D-047 has established precision, recall, priority usefulness, and false-positive behavior. [D-045](../../docs/DECISIONS.md#d-045-make-evidence-backed-deep-review-the-primary-experience); [D-047](../../docs/DECISIONS.md#d-047-validate-deep-review-with-blinded-finding-labels)
- The recommendations PRD is useful as a defect-discovery proxy and transfer fixture, not as calibration truth. Forge explicitly excludes public and synthetic cases from weight, gate, band, and headline-accuracy decisions. [Scoring theory, lines 350-380](../../docs/SCORING_THEORY.md#L350-L380); [Expert baseline, lines 27-38](../../docs/EXPERT_BASELINE.md#L27-L38)
- Existing fields should be credited only for the evidence they literally require. A long list of rules can satisfy `requirements`; it cannot, without a distinct requirement, prove canonical precedence, cross-rule consistency, or acceptance coverage. This follows Forge's field-level evidence model, where a non-null value plus a verified quote is sufficient unless an objective constraint is configured. [Extraction model, lines 45-63](../../src/forge/extract/models.py#L45-L63); [Scoring theory, lines 20-34](../../docs/SCORING_THEORY.md#L20-L34)

### Gaps

- No representative internal, independently labelled corpus establishes whether the current generic fields, gates, or bands match company reviewers. The project records that tuning terminology, weights, gates, and bands remains blocked on those inputs. [Roadmap, lines 232-242](../../docs/ROADMAP.md#L232-L242)
- The current build has not yet extended classified conflict coverage to undefined boundaries and unstated fallbacks, and candidate recall has not been measured on representative PRDs. [Roadmap, lines 191-200](../../docs/ROADMAP.md#L191-L200)
- Visual meaning remains advisory; figures or diagrams in the DOCX/PDF samples cannot safely satisfy scored fields unless their text enters the normalized, quote-verifiable evidence corpus. [Product, lines 34-38](../../docs/PRODUCT.md#L34-L38); [Scoring theory, lines 49-53](../../docs/SCORING_THEORY.md#L49-L53)

## 2. Which exact recommendations-PRD defects would current scoring miss or underweight?

### Takeaway

Current scoring will correctly identify many missing readiness fields, but it can award field presence to individually explicit rules even when the document gives incompatible definitions, states, precedence chains, or lifecycle scopes. Some numeric/timeline cases are already caught by deep review; the most important remaining misses are general semantic conflicts for which current candidate generation has no stable subject key.

## 3. What minimal rubric edits and tests would improve actionability without overfitting?

### Takeaway

Make three small, weight-neutral rubric edits: add one collision-resolution field to functional requirements, add one requirement-to-acceptance mapping field, and strengthen dependency-readiness wording to require stale/unavailable fallback evidence. Do not change criterion weights, gates, bands, or add recommendation-specific vocabulary from these synthetic proxy documents.

### Cited Findings

#### Minimal edits

1. **Add `decision_rules_and_precedence` as a required field inside `functional_requirements`; leave the criterion weight and gate unchanged.** Suggested generic description: “For behaviours with overlapping conditions, ranked inputs, or multiple applicable rules: the authoritative order, tie-break, default, and fallback; otherwise an explicit statement that no rule collision applies.” Suggested remediation question: “When two rules can apply at once, which one wins, and what default or fallback applies?” This closes the gap between current launch `prioritisation` and runtime precedence without creating a new weighted criterion. [Current functional-requirements fields](../../src/forge/rubric/library/prd.v0.yaml#L216-L241); [D-045 precedence requirement](../../docs/DECISIONS.md#d-045-make-evidence-backed-deep-review-the-primary-experience)
2. **Add `requirement_coverage` as a required field inside `acceptance_criteria`; leave the criterion weight and existing gate unchanged.** Suggested generic description: “A mapping from every launch-critical functional requirement and decision-rule boundary to at least one observable pass/fail case.” Suggested remediation question: “Which pass/fail case covers each must-have requirement and each boundary where behavior changes?” The existing `criteria` and `failure_and_boundary_criteria` fields remain useful; the new field asks for coverage rather than another example. [Current acceptance fields](../../src/forge/rubric/library/prd.v0.yaml#L243-L261); [Current field-presence semantics](../../src/forge/extract/models.py#L45-L63)
3. **Strengthen, rather than duplicate, `dependencies.dependency_readiness`.** Suggested description: “For each material team, service, vendor, model, or data source: owner, interface/readiness condition, freshness or availability expectation where applicable, failure impact, and stale/unavailable fallback.” Suggested remediation question: “Who owns each material dependency, what proves it ready, and what happens when it is late, stale, or unavailable?” The present field already asks for owner, readiness, failure impact, and fallback, so this is a wording refinement, not a new score dimension. [Current dependency field](../../src/forge/rubric/library/prd.v0.yaml#L320-L341)
4. **Do not modify weights, gates, or band thresholds from this panel.** Forge permits those changes only against representative PRDs with independent human document-level labels; public and synthetic documents may expand format and adversarial coverage but cannot serve as calibration truth. 5. **Do not encode `mood`, `skip`, `MAB`, `Data Saver`, quality ladders, recommendation sources, Xstream, or other sample vocabulary in the rubric.** Those are excellent fixtures for generic collision, boundary, dependency, and supersession behavior, but the bundled rubric is explicitly cross-industry. ### Inferences

- These edits are minimal because they add two evidence slots to existing criteria and refine one existing field; they do not introduce a criterion, weight, gate, band, or company-specific term. Their effect is limited to whether existing functional, acceptance, and dependency criteria become `present` versus `partial`. [Scoring theory, lines 20-34 and 67-78](../../docs/SCORING_THEORY.md#L20-L34)
- A future stronger guarantee would need requirement-by-acceptance and rule-collision ledgers analogous to D-038. YAML field presence alone cannot mechanically prove cardinality or consistency; it can only require the document to contain an explicit mapping or canonical rule. [D-038](../../docs/DECISIONS.md#d-038-score-edge-cases-from-a-versioned-coverage-ledger); [Extraction model, lines 45-63](../../src/forge/extract/models.py#L45-L63)
- The most defensible implementation order is: rubric wording/field tests first, D-045 generic candidate expansion second, blinded D-047 evaluation third, and only then consideration of any additional scored criterion. This preserves the product rule that model-produced labels are synthetic proxy labels until independent human review exists. [Roadmap, lines 191-215](../../docs/ROADMAP.md#L191-L215); [D-047](../../docs/DECISIONS.md#d-047-validate-deep-review-with-blinded-finding-labels)

### Gaps

- The proposed field names and wording have not been tested with independent authors or reviewers; usability and extraction stability remain hypotheses until evaluated. [Scoring theory, lines 350-380](../../docs/SCORING_THEORY.md#L350-L380)
- A field-level `requirement_coverage` quote may still be overstated by a model because Forge currently verifies quote existence, not completeness of the mapping. A ledger is required for a mechanical completeness claim. [Extraction model, lines 45-63](../../src/forge/extract/models.py#L45-L63); [D-038](../../docs/DECISIONS.md#d-038-score-edge-cases-from-a-versioned-coverage-ledger)
- No numeric weight, threshold, expected score movement, or predicted reduction in rework is supportable from these three source documents. Forge explicitly rejects such unvalidated predictive claims. [Product, lines 67-79](../../docs/PRODUCT.md#L67-L79); [Scoring theory, lines 309-318](../../docs/SCORING_THEORY.md#L309-L318)
